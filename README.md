## 🎵 What is MUSIC CUP?

Ever argued with friends over which track is your favorite artist's true GOAT? Or stared at an album wondering which song would actually win it all?

**MUSIC CUP turns that daydream into a tournament.** Pick any artist, and the app builds a full **World Cup–style bracket** from their songs — group stage, a wildcard play-off, then a single-elimination knockout — until one track lifts the trophy. 🏆

It's personal, addictive, and ridiculously fun. Every pairing is a tiny heartbreak: _Cruel Summer or Lover? Bohemian Rhapsody or Somebody to Love?_ Tap the one you love more, round after round, until your all-time favorite song is crowned the champion.

## ⚽ How it works

A real World Cup — but for songs:

1. **Pick your artist** — search anyone, or start with a one-tap kickoff (Taylor Swift, The Weeknd, Billie Eilish…).
2. **Choose your song pool** — Top Hits, Random Picks, or hand-pick every track yourself (deep cuts welcome 🎚️).
3. **Group Stage** — songs are drawn into groups; two advance from each.
4. **Wildcard Play-off** — the near-misses get one last shot at glory.
5. **Knockout Rounds** — 16 → 8 → 4 → 2, head-to-head all the way to the final.
6. **Champion crowned** 🎉 — confetti, a champion / runner-up / semi-finalists podium, and a bracket worth sharing.

## ✨ Why you'll love it

- 🎧 **Preview before you pick** — tap any song to hear a 30-second snippet. Torn between two? Hit play and let your ears decide.
- 🎲 **Three ways to play** — chart-toppers, a random shuffle, or your own curated lineup. Bring on the B-sides and fan favorites.
- 🔍 **Any artist, any era** — if they have a catalog, they have a World Cup waiting.
- 🏆 **A bracket worth sharing** — generate a beautiful "road to the title" image complete with a QR code. _"I hosted a World Cup for Taylor Swift — the champion is Cruel Summer."_
- 🌍 **Speaks your language** — fully localized in 14 languages, with homegrown artist suggestions for each (BTS in 한국어, 米津玄師 in 日本語, Bad Bunny in Español…).
- ⚡ **Instant fun** — open the link, search, and you're playing in seconds.

## 🎉 Ready to crown your champion?

👉 Head to **[musiccup.org](https://musiccup.org)**, search your favorite artist, and let the tournament begin.

May the best song win. 👑
